System architecture

Capture on the wrist. Understand with the phone.

The wearable timestamps audio and sensor data. The companion app links those streams into a personal timeline. Transcription and AI can run through an explicitly enabled cloud path; the band does not run a large language model.

01 / Device

Keep the raw streams aligned

  • 16 kHz mono speech capture while Active.
  • 64 kbps ADPCM target; 70 kbps storage budget with framing.
  • PPG with signal-quality flags and motion context.
  • Monotonic clock plus phone UTC anchors.
  • Encrypted, sequenced chunks with resumable transfer.
02 / Phone

Join memory and wellness

  • Pairing, privacy state, local buffering and deletion controls.
  • UTC alignment without inventing missing sensor readings.
  • Summaries linked to transcript evidence and time ranges.
  • Health correlations labeled as associations, not causes.
  • BLE background behavior tested on iOS and Android.
03 / Services

Keep the user in control

  • Explicit permission before cloud processing.
  • Separate raw audio, transcript and derived-memory retention.
  • Deletion propagates to indexes and derived records.
  • No biometric identity or medical diagnosis claimed.
  • No fabricated stress score from a conversation alone.

Engineering estimates

Audio defines the battery budget.

An adjustable planning model, not measured performance. Capacity edits do not resize the CAD. Continuous optical sampling, RF conditions and encoding choices can change these estimates substantially.

Assumptions: 9 mA capture + wellness, 1.4 mA wellness-only. Active current includes nominal BLE transfer. No tested battery or firmware exists in this package.
