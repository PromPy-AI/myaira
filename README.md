# AIRA: Future Wellness

Build AIRA — Minimal Premium Landing Page

Create a very simple, extremely minimal, premium landing page for AIRA, a new wearable technology company.

The website should feel like a premium future-facing wellness technology brand, not a typical startup landing page.

Use the current Temple website (https://www.temple.com/) as a visual reference for its overall feeling: sophisticated, minimal, editorial, spacious, confident, premium, and slightly mysterious.

Do not copy Temple's branding, exact layout, copy, assets, or design. Take inspiration only from the principles of the visual experience.

Also use the attached reference image as inspiration for the gradient treatment, typography scale, negative space, and premium editorial aesthetic.

1. CORE BRAND

Brand

AIRA

AIRA is a wearable that helps people understand themselves today while preserving the memories, voice, stories, and personality that make them who they are.

The long-term vision is that the more someone uses AIRA, the more accurately it understands their life and can preserve their legacy for the people they love.

Do NOT make the website feel like a "death technology" website.

Do NOT lead with death, grief, immortality, or "talk to dead people."

The first impression should be:

premium wellness technology + personal intelligence + memory + legacy

The deeper concept should reveal itself gradually.

2. WEBSITE GOAL

This is NOT the full AIRA website.

This is a small first-version landing page designed primarily to:

Introduce AIRA.

Create curiosity.

Communicate the philosophy.

Show that something significant is being built.

Collect early-access / waitlist signups.

Keep the page intentionally short.

Do not add unnecessary sections.

Do not create a huge SaaS-style landing page.

Do not create pricing tables.

Do not create a detailed product comparison.

Do not create a long FAQ.

Do not create a complicated navigation system.

The feeling should be:

"There is something important coming."

3. VISUAL DIRECTION

The design should be:

Extremely minimal

Premium

Calm

Sophisticated

Editorial

Futuristic without looking sci-fi

Warm rather than cold

Human rather than clinical

High-end consumer technology

Lots of negative space

Large typography

Very subtle animations

No visual clutter

Think:

Apple × high-end wellness × luxury editorial × future technology

But do NOT make it look like Apple, Oura, WHOOP, or any existing wearable brand.

AIRA should have its own identity.

4. PRIMARY COLOR PALETTE

The main website gradient must be built around these exact colors:

Primary palette

#D6BD98

#677D6A

#40534C

#1A3636

Use these as a sophisticated multi-stop gradient.

The gradient should feel:

warm champagne / muted beige → sage → deep desaturated green → deep forest

Do NOT make the gradient look bright, colorful, or "Instagram-like."

It should be subtle and luxurious.

The gradient can move diagonally or organically across the hero.

Suggested visual direction:

Top/upper area: dark forest / deep green

Transition: muted green

Middle: sage

Lower or focal transition: warm champagne/beige

Use very soft transitions.

Avoid hard gradient bands.

The background should feel almost atmospheric.

5. HERO SECTION

The hero should occupy most of the first viewport.

Very little content.

At the top:

AIRA

Small, elegant wordmark.

Navigation can be extremely minimal:

About
Technology
Join Waitlist

But keep navigation subtle.

If a navigation bar makes the page feel less premium, reduce it to:

AIRA on the left
Join Waitlist on the right

That's enough.

6. HERO COPY

Use large, elegant typography.

Primary headline:

Remember what makes you, you.

Alternative supporting line:

AIRA is a wearable that learns from your life — your health, your voice, your stories, and the moments that matter.

Keep this text short.

The headline should be very large.

Do not use generic startup language such as:

"Revolutionizing the future of wearable technology."

Do not use:

"AI-powered."

Do not use:

"The world's first..."

Do not use excessive buzzwords.

AIRA should feel confident enough not to explain everything immediately.

7. HERO VISUAL

Create a very subtle premium visual treatment behind or beside the hero.

If product imagery is not available, do NOT create a fake generic smartwatch.

Instead use:

abstract atmospheric gradient

subtle light

soft metallic texture

extremely subtle grain

faint curved forms suggesting a wearable

elegant shadow/highlight

The visual should hint at technology without screaming "technology."

If an AIRA device image is available later, the hero should be designed so it can easily be inserted without redesigning the entire page.

8. SECOND SECTION — THE IDEA

After the hero, create one very spacious section.

Small eyebrow:

A LIFE, REMEMBERED.

Large statement:

The longer you wear it, the more of you it remembers.

Supporting copy:

AIRA quietly builds a private understanding of your life over time — from the conversations you choose to preserve to the moments, experiences, and patterns that make you uniquely you.

Keep this section visually sparse.

No cards.

No icon grid.

No unnecessary illustrations.

One powerful statement.

9. THIRD SECTION — THREE SIMPLE IDEAS

Create a very minimal three-part section.

Three columns on desktop, stacked on mobile.

01 — LIVE

Understand yourself today.

Health and wellness insights designed to help you understand your body, activity, sleep, stress, and everyday patterns.

02 — REMEMBER

Preserve what matters.

AIRA builds a private, evolving memory of the moments, conversations, stories, and experiences you choose to keep.

03 — LEGACY

Leave something behind.

Your memories can become part of a legacy you intentionally leave for the people who matter most.

Keep these extremely short.

No large icons.

Use typography and spacing as the primary visual system.

10. PRIVACY SECTION

This is important to the AIRA brand.

Create a very small, elegant section with a dark background.

Eyebrow:

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Headline:

Your life. Your data. Your decision.

Supporting text:

AIRA is designed around control. Pause it. Mute it. Block it. Delete what you don't want remembered. Decide what stays, what goes, and who can access your legacy.

Then show three tiny text controls:

ACTIVE
Remember

MUTE
Pause

BLOCK
Don't remember

These should look like refined UI elements, not large colorful buttons.

This section should communicate that privacy is a fundamental product principle.

11. FINAL CTA

At the bottom, create a very spacious final section.

Headline:

Something worth remembering is coming.

Supporting copy:

Join the early access list and be among the first to experience AIRA.

Then a simple email field:

Enter your email

Button:

Join Early Access

Keep the CTA extremely clean.

No aggressive "BUY NOW."

No countdown timer.

No fake urgency.

No discount.

No pricing.

12. FOOTER

Extremely minimal.

AIRA

Small text:

© 2026 AIRA. All rights reserved.

Links:

Privacy

Terms

Contact

Nothing else.

13. TYPOGRAPHY

Typography is extremely important.

Use a premium modern sans-serif.

Preferred options:

Inter

Geist

Helvetica Neue

Neue Haas Grotesk-style alternative

Instrument Sans

Choose one font family and use it consistently.

The typography should have:

Large hero headline

Very light/regular body copy

Tight but elegant letter spacing

Excellent line height

Strong hierarchy

Do NOT use overly futuristic fonts.

Do NOT use rounded startup fonts.

Do NOT use excessive bold text.

The typography should feel expensive.

14. LAYOUT

Use a very wide desktop layout with generous margins.

Maximum content width:

~1200–1400px

Hero should have significant vertical breathing room.

Use:

large margins

large section spacing

very few elements

asymmetry where appropriate

strong typography

Do not fill empty space unnecessarily.

Negative space is part of the design.

The page should feel almost slightly "empty" in a deliberate way.

15. ANIMATIONS

Animations should be extremely subtle.

Use:

slow gradient movement

gentle opacity transitions

subtle text reveal

very slight parallax if appropriate

smooth hover transitions

Avoid:

bouncing elements

spinning 3D objects

flashy animations

particle effects

excessive scroll animations

cursor-following effects

loud transitions

The website should feel calm.

16. RESPONSIVENESS

The page must look excellent on:

Desktop

Laptop

Tablet

Mobile

On mobile:

Preserve the large typography

Keep the page spacious

Stack the three concept sections

Keep navigation minimal

Make the email form easy to use

Never allow the design to become cramped

The mobile version should feel intentionally designed, not like a compressed desktop page.

17. UI STYLE

Buttons should be understated.

Use:

subtle rounded corners

thin borders

elegant hover states

no gradients inside buttons unless absolutely necessary

The primary CTA can use a light/cream button against the dark gradient or a dark forest button against the warm background.

Keep contrast accessible.

18. IMPORTANT — DO NOT OVERBUILD

This is the most important instruction.

Do not turn this into a large marketing website.

I want a small, premium, almost editorial landing page.

Think:

One beautiful page.

Not:

A startup website with 15 sections.

The first version should be approximately:

Navigation

Hero

Philosophy

Live / Remember / Legacy

Privacy

Early access CTA

Footer

That's it.

19. BRAND FEEL

When someone lands on AIRA, they should think:

"What is this?"

Then:

"This feels extremely premium."

Then:

"This is something different."

Then:

"I want to know more."

The website should create curiosity rather than explain everything immediately.

AIRA should feel like a product from the near future that already has a strong identity.

20. DO NOT USE THESE VISUAL ELEMENTS

Avoid:

Stock photos

Generic people smiling at cameras

Generic smartwatch renders

Medical imagery

DNA graphics

Brain graphics

AI robot imagery

Circuit-board backgrounds

Neon blue/purple AI gradients

Excessive glassmorphism

Huge glowing 3D spheres

Generic SaaS cards

Excessive icons

Trust-badge walls

Fake testimonials

Fake statistics

Fake customer logos

The brand should feel confident without needing these.

21. TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION

Build the website as a polished responsive React/Next.js-style landing page.

Requirements:

Clean component structure

Responsive design

Fast loading

SEO-friendly metadata

Accessible semantic HTML

Optimized typography

Smooth scrolling

Proper keyboard accessibility

Good mobile performance

No unnecessary dependencies

Create the email waitlist form visually and structure it so a real backend/form provider can be connected later.

For now, the form can use a simple placeholder submission handler, but make the component easy to connect to Supabase, Resend, or another backend later.

22. FINAL DESIGN TEST

Before considering the design complete, ask:

Does this look like a premium $500M+ technology company could have designed it?

If not, simplify it.

Remove things rather than adding things.

The visual hierarchy should be driven by:

Typography → space → gradient → subtle interaction

not:

cards → icons → illustrations → gradients → badges → buttons everywhere.

The website should be quiet, premium, emotional, mysterious, and technologically credible.

The final impression should be:

AIRA

Remember what makes you, you.
